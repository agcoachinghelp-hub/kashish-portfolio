A north-facing facade design for a couple living in a small double-storey house near the Northern Beaches, directly opposite a public park. Active and health conscious, they love walks in nature, waking up to natural light, growing their own food and filling their home with plants.

- North-facing facade with high sun exposure, the driest face of the building
- Opposite a public park, balancing privacy with interaction with nature
- Flat roof for planting
- Skylight void to emphasise the permeability and openness of the facade structure
- Half-level bedroom with a balcony, connecting both levels and the views
