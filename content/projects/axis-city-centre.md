*Aligning movement, people & space.*

Axis Waterloo City Centre is designed as an inclusive civic hub that prioritises accessibility, connection and belonging. Organised around a central axis, the project creates a clear movement spine that links public spaces, built areas and landscape zones into one coherent experience.

A key aim is accessibility for all. The design supports people of different ages, abilities and needs through open circulation, clear pathways and spaces that are easy to navigate. The centre also integrates with the natural topography of the site, using stepped platforms, terraces and layered levels to create a building that responds to the land rather than flattening it.

Each space is designed to offer a distinct and interesting experience. The open communal areas encourage gathering and interaction, while quieter zones provide places for rest and reflection. Terraces create elevated views across the site, shaded walkways make movement comfortable, and landscaped pockets soften the built form. Dedicated spaces for families, workers, students, visitors and community groups ensure the centre remains flexible and inclusive.

Overall, Axis Waterloo City Centre aims to become a vibrant, human-centred place where people can meet, move, relax, work, learn and stay.
