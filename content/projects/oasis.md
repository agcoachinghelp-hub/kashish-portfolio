*Aligning movement, people & space.*

This project is derived from the radical model "Held in the Hands of Time", which explores how water and time shape form. Water flow acts as both a metaphor and a spatial organiser, guiding visitors through a hydrological experience of pooling, meandering and releasing: a circulation that unfolds as a slow journey, engaging with the Coal Loader's industrial past through eroded forms and carved voids, showing how architecture is also shaped by time.
